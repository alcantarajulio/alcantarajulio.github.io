This guide follows the 424CIT module, and it is the only one of the three where security
is not the central subject. It covers operations and resource management, with one
chapter on cyber security seen through the eyes of whoever administers rather than
whoever defends.

**Before you start.** The content is theoretical and needs no software. Each chapter
closes with a practice section applying the framework to a case, and the module's
supporting material remains the property of Coventry University, so none of it is
reproduced here.

Six chapters: operations strategy, performance and legislation, health and safety, cyber
security in management, technology use and impact, and emerging technologies.
